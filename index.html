02 // ABOUT

Frontend developer with a taste for interactive details.

I'm Muhammad Nawal Fajar Riyadi, a frontend developer who works under the name KUROZ4PH. I enjoy turning ideas into interfaces people can explore: responsive websites, creative frontend experiments and digital tools.

I'm the founder of Deraclas Community, and several of the projects below were built for it. I like to keep things hands-on: build it, try a new technique, then refine it.

Focus areas

  • Interactive interfaces UI that responds to the person using it, with motion, feedback and thoughtful states.
  • Responsive websites Layouts that hold up from small Android screens to large desktop displays.
  • Creative experiments Canvas, animation and shader-flavoured ideas explored in small prototypes.
  • Digital tools Practical web apps and community utilities, from catalogs to bots.
